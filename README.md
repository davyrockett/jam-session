# Jam Session

Music activities for sessions, built for the iPad.

**Open it:** https://davyrockett.github.io/jam-session/
On the iPad, open it in Safari, tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**. It then works like an app, even without wifi.

## What's in it

- **Songbook** – picture books of the songs written in sessions (starting with *Walter's Cruise*).
  Turn pages with the big Back / Next buttons or by swiping the picture. On the Walter's Cruise
  cover, Walter picks the colour of the boat and every boat in the book changes.
  **Music** plays a laid-back island groove in G major (to match Walter's Hapi drum) that follows the verse
  and chorus. Leave it off when you're playing guitar.
- **Cause & Effect** – every hit on a drum, shaker or tambourine makes something happen: fish jump, animals
  pop up over the fence, cars drive by, fireworks go off. Tap **Listen** to use the microphone; tapping the
  picture works too. If it reacts to talking (or your guitar), lower the **Sensitivity**. The orange bar shows
  what the iPad hears; the black line is how loud a hit needs to be.
- **Song Builder** – the client picks pictures: what we saw, its colour, what it did, how it felt. The app
  writes a 4-page story song. Change any words, then **Save to Songbook**. Saved songs have an **Edit** button
  (where you can also delete them). To keep them on every device, turn on sync in Settings (below).
- **Hello / Goodbye** – the session songs as picture books. Type the client's first name on the first page and
  it appears in the words. Names are never saved.
- **Choice Board** – pick 2 to 4 activities, tap **Show choices**, and the client picks what's next.

## Settings (gear)

Light / Dark / Auto, music speed, sync, and Check for updates.

**Sync Song Builder songs:** songs you build are saved in your private GitHub project
`jam-session-data`, so every device shows the same songs. Set it up once on each device by pasting your
access key in Settings (the steps are under "How to get an access key"). The app syncs whenever it opens.

## Adding a song you wrote together

Ask Claude: "Add a song to Jam Session" and give the lyrics. The pictures are drawn in code, so describe what
should be in each picture.
